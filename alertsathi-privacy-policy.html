AlertSathi Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 July 2026

AlertSathi is an offline reminder and daily planner app. The current version does not require a login, password, OTP or mandatory online account. Core reminder information is stored locally on the user's device.

1. Information Stored on Your Device

AlertSathi may locally store information entered by the user, including:

  • Task and reminder titles
  • Dates and times
  • Notes and descriptions
  • Names, phone numbers or addresses entered in a reminder
  • Payment amounts or collection details entered by the user
  • Task priority and completion status
  • Selected user roles
  • Language and notification preferences
  • Repeat, sound, vibration and snooze settings

This information is normally stored locally on the user's device so that reminders can work without a mandatory backend server.

2. Information We Do Not Sell

AlertSathi does not sell users' personal information to advertisers, data brokers or other third parties.

3. Permissions Used

AlertSathi may request the following Android permissions:

  • Notifications: Used to show scheduled task and reminder alerts.
  • Alarm and scheduling access: Used to deliver reminders at the date and time selected by the user.
  • Vibration: Used when the user selects vibration for a reminder.
  • Audio: Used to play the reminder tone selected by the user.
  • Files or storage: Used only when the user selects an available backup, export or restore feature.

4. How Information Is Used

Information is used only to:

  • Create and organise tasks and reminders
  • Show local notifications at the selected time
  • Support repeat reminders and snooze options
  • Play sound or vibration according to user settings
  • Display task completion status and local history
  • Save preferences selected by the user
  • Maintain app stability and resolve technical issues

5. Notifications and Reminders

AlertSathi displays notifications based on reminders created by the user. Users can manage notification permission through Android settings. Reminder sound, vibration, snooze and repeat settings can be changed where those options are available in the app.

6. Backup and Export

When an export, backup or restore feature is available, the app accesses files only after the user chooses that action. Users are responsible for protecting exported backup files and should not share them with untrusted persons.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Locally stored tasks, reminders and settings remain on the device until the user deletes them, clears the app's data or uninstalls the app.

Users may delete individual tasks or clear available history through the app. For privacy questions or deletion assistance, contact the Developer and Grievance Officer using the information below.

8. Data Security

AlertSathi uses reasonable security measures appropriate for a local Android application. Users should protect their device with a password, PIN, fingerprint or other screen-lock method.

9. Children's Privacy

AlertSathi is a general productivity application and is not specifically directed to children. A parent or guardian who believes a child has provided personal information may contact us for assistance.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when AlertSathi features, permissions or legal requirements change. The latest update date will be displayed at the top of this page.
