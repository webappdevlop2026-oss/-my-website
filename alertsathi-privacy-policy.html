AlertSathi Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 July 2026

AlertSathi is an offline reminder and daily work planner. It does not require an account, login, backend server, or cloud database.

1. Information Stored on Your Device

AlertSathi may store reminder titles, dates, times, names, phone numbers, addresses, notes, payment amounts, task status, selected roles, language preferences, and notification settings. This information is stored locally on the user's device.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

AlertSathi does not collect, upload, sell, or share personal information with the developer or third parties.

  • No account or login information is collected.
  • No reminder data is uploaded to a server.
  • No advertising identifier is collected.
  • No analytics or tracking SDK is used.

3. App Permissions

AlertSathi may request the following permissions only to provide its core features:

  • Notifications: To show reminders at the selected time.
  • Alarms and reminders: To schedule reminders accurately.
  • Vibration: To vibrate when a reminder is triggered.
  • Boot completed: To restore scheduled reminders after the phone restarts.
  • Foreground service: To play the reminder bell when a reminder is active.

4. Phone, Maps, and External Apps

AlertSathi may open the phone dialer or Google Maps only when the user taps the related button. AlertSathi does not place calls automatically and does not track the user's live location.

5. Backup and Restore

Users may export an AlertSathi backup file and choose where to save or share it. The developer cannot access the backup file. Users are responsible for keeping exported backup files secure.

6. Data Deletion

Users can delete individual reminders or clear all AlertSathi data from the app settings. Uninstalling the app also removes locally stored app data unless the user separately saved a backup file.

7. Children's Privacy

AlertSathi does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because the app works offline and does not transmit user data, no personal information is sent to the developer.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This policy may be updated when AlertSathi features or permissions change. The latest version will always be available on this page.
