Privacy Policy

AlertSathi is an offline-first Android planner and reminder application. It helps users create tasks, schedules and reminders, receive local notifications, manage repeat alerts and snooze options, and review recent activity. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be processed and how it is handled.

No login, password or OTP is required for the current version of AlertSathi. Core task and reminder information is stored locally on the user's device.

Information Processed

  • Task and reminder information: task title, description, date, time, repeat settings, priority, reminder tone and completion status entered by the user.
  • Optional role preferences: roles or categories selected by the user, such as Student, Teacher, Business, Driver, Field Worker, Shop Owner, Office Worker or Personal Use.
  • Local app settings: notification mode, vibration, sound, snooze, language or other preferences selected in the app.
  • Technical information: limited app-version, device and diagnostic information needed for normal operation and troubleshooting.

Where Data Is Stored

AlertSathi is designed to work without a mandatory online account or backend. Tasks, reminders and preferences are generally stored locally on the user's device. AlertSathi does not sell users' personal information.

If the app provides an export, backup or restore option, files are created or selected only when the user chooses that action. Users are responsible for keeping exported backup files secure.

Permissions and Device Access

  • Notifications: used to display scheduled reminders and task alerts.
  • Alarm and scheduling access: may be used to deliver reminders at the selected time, including urgent reminders where supported by the device.
  • Vibration and audio: used only according to the reminder mode and tone chosen by the user.
  • Files or storage: used only when the user chooses an available export, backup or restore action.

How Information Is Used

  • to create and organise tasks and reminders;
  • to schedule local notifications at the chosen time;
  • to support repeat reminders, snooze, sound and vibration settings;
  • to show task history, completion status or local reports;
  • to save user-selected app preferences;
  • to maintain app stability, security and troubleshooting.

Notifications

AlertSathi may display reminder notifications based on schedules created by the user. Users can manage notification permission through Android settings and may change sound, vibration, snooze or repeat options inside the app where available.

Third-Party Sharing

AlertSathi does not sell personal information. Information may be shared only when required by law, needed to protect users or the app, or processed by essential Android system services needed to deliver notifications and alarms.

Data Retention and Deletion

Locally stored tasks, reminders and history remain on the device until the user deletes them, clears app data or uninstalls the app. Users can delete individual tasks or clear available history through the app where those controls are provided.

For privacy questions or deletion assistance, users may contact the Developer and Grievance Officer using the details below.

Security

AlertSathi uses reasonable measures appropriate for a local Android application. Users should keep their device protected with a screen lock and avoid sharing exported backup files with untrusted persons.

Children's Privacy

AlertSathi is a general productivity application and is not specifically directed to children. A parent or guardian who believes a child has submitted personal information may contact us for assistance.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when app features, permissions or legal requirements change. The latest effective date will be displayed at the top of this page.
